Field notes

Pick a summon below and tap a lit tile near the ward. The ward holds as long as nothing reaches it.

Summons

Wisp floats through anything. Alone it pokes at enemies; once other summons are around, it drifts to them and mends their bones instead. Wisps find swamps irresistible and sometimes wander off to admire one.

Skeleton walks around obstacles and hits hard. Archer shoots from two tiles away but is slow and fragile.

Spells

Command calls every summon toward a tile for a second. Nemesis marks one enemy for five seconds, and every summon goes after it.

Enemies

Kobolds scatter whenever one of their own drops. Goblins lose their nerve when four fall in quick succession, unless an Orc is nearby to shout at them. From wave 10, they arrive in tight packs.

Terrain

Sand and puddles slow everyone a little, swamp a lot. Bushes slow enemies only. Boulders, trees, lakes and rivers stop anyone on foot; cross rivers by the bridges.

Bones you collect here turn into a little extra experience at the end. Bought bones are used only after the ones you find.
