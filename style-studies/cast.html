The Summonancer · style study

The cast so far

Two summons and three enemies, side by side and in sync. Skeletons never panic, so in Panic mode they just keep walking; the wisp is more excitable. Charmed shows what any character looks like under a charm: hearts for eyes. Confused tilts heads, raises an eyebrow and sends question marks flying. Every skeleton rolls its own missing teeth, one or two glowing eyes and a few skull cracks, every goblin its gold rings, every kobold the side its tongue hangs from and maybe a lost horn and every orc its scars and plasters.
