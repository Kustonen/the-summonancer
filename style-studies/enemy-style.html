The Summonancer · style study

Goblin, Kobold, Orc

The same build as the skeletons: a big head, a tiny torso, and floating hands and feet with no limbs in between. Each enemy gets one clear trait you can read from across the battlefield: the goblin's ears and grin, the kobold's snout and tail, the orc's tusks and size.

Animation

Goblin

Wide grin, crooked teeth, ears that flop with every step. Attacks by poking with a rusty little knife.

Kobold

Smaller and quicker, with a snout, stubby horns and a tail that never stops wagging. Attacks with a claw swipe and yaps as it walks.

Orc

Bigger, slower and grumpier. Heavy brow, underbite, tusks. Attacks by raising both fists and slamming them down.
