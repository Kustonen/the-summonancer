Every enemy currently in the game, drawn by the same code the battlefield and the Grimoire use: each animation, each attack move, and a few randomly rolled looks. Graphics for the newest creatures are first versions.

Raid chiefs

The bosses at the end of the Raid's red carpet: bigger, crowned and never scared. In battle they also get their own health bar along the top of the field.

Stats are the base values on Normal fields before wave scaling. Tap “Roll new looks” to see more of the random variety: missing teeth and horns, scars and plasters, belt skulls and forehead horns, nose rings, earrings, moss and hair colours.
