The Summonancer · style study

Skeleton Warrior, two takes

Chibi proportions, thick outlines, flat colours. The skull is most of the skeleton, the upper teeth are part of it, the eye sockets don't match, and the lower jaw drops open on every step, swaying left and right in turn. No arms or legs, just floating hands that pump the air and feet that shuffle under a tiny torso. Left: empty sockets. Right: the same sockets with small glowing pupils that don't quite agree on where to look.

Empty sockets

Reads as blank and a bit dim. Expression comes from the jaw and the wobble alone.

Glowing pupils

A spark of life. The pupils point slightly different ways, which adds a cross-eyed, eager look.

Three ways to hit

Each swing winds up, snaps forward and recovers. In the Attack mode above, every swing picks one of the three at random.

Every skeleton is a little different

In the game each summoned skeleton would roll 1–4 missing teeth when it appears. It's only looks: no effect on health or damage.

Animation
  • Outlines are thick and slightly warm-dark, never pure black, so they sit well on the night battlefield.
  • The skull tilts a little with each step and the body bobs, which keeps a very simple drawing feeling alive. The lower jaw always hangs below the skull, so it never disappears behind it.
  • Everything is drawn in code, so the same skeleton scales to any screen and can swap parts (helmet, bow, colours) for the other summons.
