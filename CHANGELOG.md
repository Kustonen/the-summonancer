# Changelog

Versions follow MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH. Before the first release everything is 0.x: the middle number goes up with every content package, the last with fixes in between. 1.0.0 will be the first store release. Versions 0.1–0.5 were numbered afterwards.

## 0.8.8-alpha – 2026-10-29
- Trolls: a bigger lower jaw that juts out under the nose and drops open when they attack or panic; the fangs grow out of it

## 0.8.7-alpha – 2026-10-29
- Trolls: fangs jutting up from the lower jaw, a pair at the corners of the mouth or just one on either side

## 0.8.6-alpha – 2026-10-29
- Ogres: no more warts; instead some carry a trophy skull swinging from the rope belt, and some have a single horn in the middle of the forehead

## 0.8.5-alpha – 2026-10-29
- Troll teeth: a more curved fang. Orc hairs: a fourth strand lying across the others

## 0.8.4-alpha – 2026-10-29
- New icon for troll teeth: a curved fang

## 0.8.3-alpha – 2026-10-29
- New icon for orc hairs: a few scraggly brown strands

## 0.8.2-alpha – 2026-10-29
- DEV console: every resource that can be dropped or forged can be set, as a compact grid of pixel icons (name on hover)

## 0.8.1-alpha – 2026-10-29
- Rapid Slash costs 750 gold + 20 iron ingots

## 0.8.0-alpha – 2026-10-29
- The Chrono Crystal (Builder, 1000 gold + 100 crystal shards): a Speed button on every battlefield switches between normal and double speed; the choice is remembered
- Academy skills cost much more: Flaming Arrows 600 gold + 15 crystal shards, Ground Slam 600 gold + 100 smooth stone + 15 crystal shards, Rapid Slash and Frost Arrows 1200 gold + 30 crystal shards + 20 iron ingots, Pseudo Reality 1500 gold + 40 crystal shards + 10 skulls (each, for wisps and for spirits). Skills already learned stay learned

## 0.7.4-alpha – 2026-10-29
- Once the Spirit Stones are built, the Wisping Well's icon gets a ring of standing stones around it

## 0.7.3-alpha – 2026-10-29
- The Builder notes that the Spirit Stones will expand the Wisping Well; once built, the well's tile reads "Stony Wisping Well"

## 0.7.2-alpha – 2026-10-29
- The Spirit Stones are now the Wisping Well's second tier instead of a building of their own: once built, the well becomes "Wisping Well with Spirit Stones", gets a second quote and shows the spirit research under the wisp research

## 0.7.1-alpha – 2026-10-29
- Fix: when the Blacksmith finished something, prices and Build / Learn / Buy buttons in town didn't notice until the town was redrawn (for example, the Invisible Academy stayed greyed out with enough iron ingots). Typed amounts at the Blacksmith now survive the refresh

## 0.7.0-alpha – 2026-10-29
- Version number in the game (bottom of the town, and in the battle field notes) and this changelog
- Pseudo Reality: a skill for wisps and for spirits, learned separately at the Invisible Academy: 10 % of physical attacks aimed at them miss. Barely There (Wisping Well, Spirit Stones) raises it 2 % a level, up to 20 %
- New drops: pouches of sand and red, blue, yellow and green mushrooms, plus one trophy each enemy carries (about 5 %): kobold scales, goblin claws, orc hairs, ogre toenails and troll teeth. Saved for alchemy and gear upgrades later; the Marketplace buys them cheap and never sells them (sand it does sell). Quests can ask for them
- The warehouse is grouped (basics, materials, forged, mushrooms, trophies) and only shows what has turned up
- Gauntlet and Raid: the wizard is the same size as elsewhere and stands in the middle tile of the 3 x 3 ward. Move only needs the wizard's own tile to be clear: the ward may slide over walls, rocks and stalagmites (enemies that touch it still hurt it), but not over a gate
- Fix: older saves without the Overdue Library could fail to load

## 0.6.0-alpha – 2026-10-29
- Blacksmith with a real-time work queue: charcoal, iron ingots and steel ingots; orders can count on what earlier jobs will make; pause, remove and reorder; keeps working in battle and while the game is closed
- Spending something the Blacksmith's queue still needs asks first
- Invisible Academy: summons learn skills once and choose which to use; one skill slot each, more from their own buildings (Smarter Skeletons, Deeper Thoughts)
- Skills: Rapid Slash (skeletons), Frost Arrows (archers); Flaming Arrows and Ground Slam moved to the Academy
- Warlord's Hand-Me-Downs, 5 pieces: summons move 50 % faster while the Nemesis target lives

## 0.5.0-alpha – 2026-10-29
- Saina Lot, skeleton bard and bookkeeper, and Saina's Wagon with the ledger
- Pixel-art resource icons everywhere
- Spellbook and spell slots
- Gauntlet, Raid with chiefs on a carpet, and the Move command
- Ogres and trolls; stalagmites
- The Spirit summon and the Spirit Stones
- Equipment with rarities and five sets, shown on the wizard
- Quests for ogres, trolls, Gauntlet caves, Raid chiefs and summoning in one battle

## 0.4.0-alpha
- Tavern with story and common quests, animated villagers and Builder in conversations
- Marketplace, materials (smooth stone, scrap iron, wood, crystal shards)
- Spells: Firebolt, Freeze, Nurture, Guardstone; Piercing and Flaming Arrows
- Assault battlefield with the red enemy ward; Retreat

## 0.3.0-alpha
- Golem and the Golematorium; Grimoire pages for allies

## 0.2.0-alpha
- Town with buildings, the Builder and the Summoning Septagram; new chibi character art, wizard sequences, music and sound effects

## 0.1.0-alpha
- First playable battle: wisps, skeletons and archers against kobolds, goblins and orcs on generated fields
